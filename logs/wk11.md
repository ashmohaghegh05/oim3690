## Mini Project 4 Idea I Chose

I chose **Replay Lab — The Study Time Machine**.

The extension will help learners save a confusing section of a YouTube video, write a note about it, replay the section, and mark it understood. It will store study information separately for each video so learners can return to their notes later.

## What I Considered When Choosing

I wanted an idea that changes the page I am visiting and gives me something useful to do with information from that page. Replay Lab connects directly to the video: it uses the playback time to save and replay a specific section.

The project also seems achievable with the course concepts I have practiced, including JavaScript, event listeners, DOM manipulation, arrays and objects, browser storage, and a Chrome extension manifest.
