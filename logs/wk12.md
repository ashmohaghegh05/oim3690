# Week 12 Notes — API Mashup and Final Project Ideas

## What I Worked On

This week I considered how a tool could combine information from different APIs and use AI to turn that information into a useful result.

I also developed three possible ideas for my final project. Each idea starts with a particular person and a problem they might want help solving.

## API mashup

A tool for people taking a short outdoor break that takes nearby parks and water features from OpenStreetMap and the next hour’s weather forecast from Open-Meteo, and asks AI to create a five-minute observation quest using things the person could look for around them.

## Final project ideas

1. **The Third Trade** — I want to help a student organizing a dorm swap find three-person trades when everyone has something to offer but no two people can swap directly.

2. **Venture Stress Test** — I want to help a student founder compare a business assumption with what customers actually said in interviews and decide what to test next.

3. **Five-Second Poster** — I want to help a club organizer find out what students remember after seeing an event poster for five seconds, so they can decide what to change before publishing it.

## What I Noticed

The ideas focus on different kinds of help. The Third Trade is about matching people, Venture Stress Test is about deciding what to investigate, and Five-Second Poster is about checking whether a message is memorable.

The API mashup is different from these project ideas. It uses information from two sources and asks AI to turn it into a small, personalized activity.

## Next Step

I will compare the three final-project ideas by looking at who would use each one, what information it needs, and whether I can build and test it with the course tools.
